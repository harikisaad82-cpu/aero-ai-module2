# Test Bank

The full Test Bank is submitted as an editable Word document in the Stage 2 Study Guide and Test Bank package, with the questions first and an answer key at the end.

It covers the core concepts of this module:

- Hardware (see `content/hardware.md`)
- Networks (see `content/networks.md`)
- Behaviorist AI (see `content/behaviorist-ai.md`)
- How the three topics connect (see `content/connections.md`)

Question types: True/False, Multiple Choice and Open-ended, each with an answer and explanation.
