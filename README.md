# Aero AI – Hardware, Networks & Behaviorist AI

**Team:** Aero AI  
**Module:** Module 2 – Hardware, Networks & Behaviorist AI  
**Course:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science  
**Stage:** Stage 2 – Study Guide and Test Bank Development

## 1. Project Overview

Aero AI is a beginner-friendly educational project designed to introduce students to three fundamental areas of computer science and artificial intelligence:

- **Computer Hardware:** Understanding the main components of a computer and how they work together to process information.
- **Computer Networks:** Learning how devices communicate, exchange information, and connect through networks.
- **Behaviorist AI:** Exploring how intelligent agents can learn through experience, rewards, penalties, and feedback.

The project aims to explain these concepts through clear explanations, practical examples, diagrams, and learning activities.

## 2. Intended Learners

The materials are designed for beginners taking an introductory course in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science.

No previous programming, networking, or advanced mathematical knowledge is required.

## 3. Learning Objectives

By completing this module, learners should be able to:

- Identify the main components of computer hardware and explain their functions.
- Describe how computers process input and produce output.
- Explain basic networking concepts, including IP addresses, protocols, and communication between devices.
- Understand how information travels across a network.
- Define key concepts in reinforcement learning, including agents, environments, actions, states, and rewards.
- Explain how feedback can influence an AI agent's future decisions.
- Recognize how hardware, networks, and AI can work together in intelligent systems.

## 6. Stage 2 Deliverables

The main objective of Stage 2 is to transform the initial topic materials into complete, organized learning resources.

The expected deliverables are:

### A. Study Guide

A complete beginner-friendly learning document covering:

1. Computer Hardware
2. Computer Networks
3. Behaviorist AI and Reinforcement Learning

The guide should include explanations, examples, diagrams, activities, and supporting references.

### B. Test Bank

A separate editable question document designed to assess learners' understanding of all three topics.

It includes different question formats and a corresponding answer key with explanations.

### C. Supporting Materials

Additional resources that support the learning activities, such as diagrams, activity files, notebooks, code, datasets, or setup instructions where applicable.

## 9. Project Goal

The final goal of Aero AI is to provide a coherent learning experience that connects computer hardware, communication networks, and behaviorist approaches to artificial intelligence.

By studying these topics together, learners can understand how intelligent systems receive information, communicate with other devices, make decisions, and improve their behavior through feedback.
